All notable user-facing changes will be documented here. The project follows semantic versioning after the first stable release; prerelease compatibility may change.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-21

Initial public prerelease of the native macOS meeting recording, transcription, encrypted local library, playback, export, recovery, and on-device intelligence workflow.

### Added

- Apache-2.0 open-source distribution maintained by RSI Tech.
- Explicit Developer ID signing, Apple notarization, stapling, Gatekeeper, checksum, and source-export workflows for direct distribution.
- Public contribution, security, privacy, conduct, release, and trademark policies.
- Public support guidance, architecture and troubleshooting documentation, issue forms, a pull-request template, and automated dependency update configuration.
